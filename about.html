About

Freshly is back on the menu.

It used to cook dinner.

Freshly was a New York City food delivery company that brought ready-made meals to doorsteps across the contiguous United States. At its peak it sent out a million meals a week. Nestlé bought it in October 2020, and in January 2023 it shut down.

This project imagines it coming back as something lighter: smoothie kits. Whole fruit and vegetables are cut at peak, frozen fast and packed in a cup. You add milk, blend for a minute and drink it.

The identity borrows from a New York corner produce stand: crates stacked on the sidewalk, fruit cut open to show it is ripe, a doodle on the price tag.

  5. 2026Back, as smoothie kits. This is the speculative part.

The system

Six parts, one stand.

A wordmark, an orange, a palette, a typeface, a set of icons and a stack. Each one answers the pointer.

The Fresh Cut

One cut runs through everything.

The same slice sits 45% of the way down the orange and every produce icon. It runs along the row by itself; drag the slider to hold it open.
